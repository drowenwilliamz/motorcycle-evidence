DOES THE EXTRA COMPLEXITY HELP?

Tested on 2025 records excluded from training and calibration

Brier score measures probability error; lower is better. AUC measures how well the model ranks KSI above slight injuries; 0.5 is chance ranking, 1.0 is perfect. Neither measure is “percentage accurate”. The constant baseline predicts the 2024 calibration-set KSI share for everyone.

Do the predicted probabilities match observed outcomes?

Five equally sized groups ordered by predicted probability. These are held-out test results, not training results. Differences show remaining calibration error.

Which inputs does the model rely on?

Increase in probability error when each input is shuffled in 2024 calibration data. Larger values mean the fitted model relies more on that input.

Averaged over five shuffles. Correlated inputs can share predictive information. This is global model dependence, not causation or an explanation of an individual result.

A defined cohort, not every rider

Eligible police forces had at least 95% injury-based reporting and at least 20 motorcycle casualties in each training year (2021–2023). Only injury-based records from those forces enter training, calibration and testing.

This reduces one source of recording inconsistency, but limits generalisation. The target is recorded KSI within that cohort, not the adjusted KSI estimate used elsewhere on the site.

How the model was built

Logistic regression provides the simpler benchmark. The AI model contains 120 shallow boosted decision trees. Both use the same ten categorical inputs and receive probability calibration using 2024 only. Settings were fixed before the 2025 test. Records from a collision cannot cross the year-based splits.

No injury outcome, collision-severity field, severity-adjustment weight, casualty count, skid or impact information is supplied as a predictor. Training support and feature-importance checks do not use 2025 outcomes. A single later-year test does not establish future reliability.

The button evaluates the frozen fitted model locally. There is no chatbot, generated narrative or live retraining, and scenario selections are not sent to an AI service.
